focuses on outcomes of using ai tools

headline claim: ai coding tools much faster at coding, but productivity boosts are negligible at shipping code b/c of bottlenecks that require human input.

defines 3 categories of ai tools:
- autocomplete
- sync ai agents (human ai collab)
- async ai agents (run in background)

Human bottlenecks were:
- reviewing code
- deployment

show that output has been many folds (like 7 fold) boosted with sync ai agents, but that only lead to 30% more releases.

they also "suggest" that app downloads didnt change (not really a claim that can be trusted. seems to general and hand-wave magic)

to get around the bottle neck, they suggest:
- making teams smaller to focus more on code reviews
- leaders should also see where ai can help
